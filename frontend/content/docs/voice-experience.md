## Talking to Clai

Tap the **microphone** next to the question box. Your browser asks for permission to use the microphone the first time. Then just ask. When you stop speaking, Clai:

1. shows what it heard,
2. finds the answer in the agreement,
3. reads the answer aloud (you can turn this off),
4. starts listening again.

The **Aura**, the moving set of lines above the conversation, shows what's happening: listening, thinking or speaking. While you speak, its movement follows your microphone level; while Clai speaks, it follows the speech engine's word timing.

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Microphone | Mute and unmute listening |
| Speaker | Turn reading answers aloud on or off |
| Interrupt | Stop the current answer and listen again |
| End voice | Turn voice off; typing still works |

The microphone pauses while Clai is reading an answer, so it doesn't hear itself. Use **Interrupt** to cut in.

## Supported browsers

| Browser | Voice input | Answers read aloud |
|---|---|---|
| Chrome, Edge (desktop and Android) | Yes | Yes |
| Safari (macOS, iOS) | Yes | Yes |
| Firefox | No (typing works) | Yes |

Support comes from your browser's built-in speech features (the Web Speech API), so it can vary by version and language settings. When voice input isn't available, Clai doesn't show the microphone, and typing works everywhere.

## What happens to your voice

Clai never receives audio, only the text your browser recognised. But your browser may send audio to its own speech service to recognise it: **Chrome and Edge** use Google's and Microsoft's services, and **Safari** uses Apple's. If you'd rather keep everything on your device, type instead. See [Privacy and security](/docs/privacy-and-security).

## Common problems

- **"Microphone access was blocked"**: allow the microphone for this site in your browser's settings (usually the icon in the address bar), then try again.
- **"Needs an internet connection"**: Chrome's recognition works online only.
- **Clai heard only part of what you said**: speak a little more slowly, or check that the right microphone is selected.
