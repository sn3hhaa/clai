## Asking good questions

Ask the way you would ask a person who has read the agreement:

| Instead of | Try |
|---|---|
| "termination" | "How can I end this agreement?" |
| "8.1" | "What notice do I need to give?" |
| "liability" | "Is there a limit on how much I could owe?" |

Clai understands everyday wording and maps it to how agreements are written. For example, *"leave early"* finds *"termination for convenience"*, and *"cap"* finds *"limitation of liability"*. One question at a time works best.

## Follow-up questions

Short follow-ups keep their context. After *"What is the notice period?"*, you can ask *"and if they breach it?"*. Clai treats it as a question about termination for breach and finds the right clause. Follow-ups that start with *and*, *what about* or *what if*, or that say *it*, *they* or *that*, are linked to your previous question.

## Reading an answer

Every answer is one of two kinds:

- **Quoted from the agreement**: the clause that answers your question, word for word, with its clause number and page. Short clauses are quoted whole; long ones are trimmed to the most relevant sentence.
- **Not found in the agreement**: Clai couldn't find a passage that answers the question. When the agreement doesn't use your key words at all, Clai names them (*"It doesn't mention 'dress code'"*). It still lists the closest passages so you can check.

See [Evidence and citations](/docs/evidence-and-citations) for how this is decided.

## The document panel

Choose **Document** in the header, or click any clause reference, to open the agreement beside the conversation (full screen on phones). It has:

- the page, with the cited passage highlighted,
- page-by-page navigation,
- an outline of sections and clauses you can jump to,
- an **Evidence** tab listing every passage Clai considered for the current answer, with how closely each matched.

## Ending a session

**End session** produces a summary of the questions you asked, the clauses that answered them, the sources cited, any questions the agreement didn't answer, and the full transcript. To start over, close the document with the **×** in the header. That also removes it from memory.
